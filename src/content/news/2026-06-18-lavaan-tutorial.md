Published a free 17-tutorial site introducing structural equation modelling in R with `lavaan`,
from R basics through CFA, path analysis, mediation, multilevel, growth, moderation, and
measurement invariance. It was written for the UEA MSc in Organisational Psychology and is an
R/lavaan port of Kevin Daniels' original Mplus materials. Open licence; please cite the site
if you use it in your own teaching.
