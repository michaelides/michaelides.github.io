The mindfulness evaluation work with the College of Policing (led by Helen Fitzhugh and including Kevin Daniels, Sara Connolly, and Ebere Nasamu) has been awarded the
2024 Best Paper by *Review of Public Personnel Administration*. A nice piece of applied
work that translated a small organisational intervention into measurable gains in
wellbeing and operational performance.
