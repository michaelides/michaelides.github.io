Pleased to share the publication of our field intervention on leisure crafting with
Paraskevas Petrou and Laura den Dulk, in *Human Relations*. The study showed that helping
employees deliberately shape their non-work time improves work-related outcomes, and
that the effect is strongest for younger workers. The paper is open access via the journal
or via the DOI above.
