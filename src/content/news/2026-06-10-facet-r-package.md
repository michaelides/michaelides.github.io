Released `facet`, an R package for Generalizability Theory analyses (G-studies, D-studies,
and reliability sweeps) with frequentist (`lme4`, `aov`) and Bayesian (`brms`) estimators. It
is the result of a long-running collaboration with Duncan Jackson at King's College London, and
grew out of our frustration at how awkward G-theory was to teach and to run in practice. The
package is open source and on GitHub; please raise issues or PRs there.
